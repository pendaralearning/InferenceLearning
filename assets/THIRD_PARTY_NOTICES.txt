Infra Lab — visual and asset provenance

The four-tile brand mark is rendered by HTML/CSS in this project. Experiment bars,
grids, schedules, and token blocks are rendered by this project's JavaScript and
CSS. The social cards are generated by scripts/social-assets.py; downloadable
result cards are drawn with browser Canvas. No third-party logo, screenshot,
paper figure, stock photo, or third-party icon pack is bundled.

The project was developed with AI coding assistance.
This inventory is a provenance record, not a trademark clearance or a guarantee
that every geometric shape is unique.
